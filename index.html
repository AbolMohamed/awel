// About Me

Bridging IT & Development to Security

I'm a Junior Cybersecurity Professional focused on both offensive (Red Team) and defensive (Blue Team) security. My path began in IT support and network administration — where I learned how real infrastructure breaks and how to keep it running.

I currently serve as a Computer & Network Technician at Admas University, handling network troubleshooting, hardware maintenance, and user support. Previously, I was an IT Support Officer at the National Election Board of Ethiopia (NEBE), ensuring system uptime in a high-stakes national environment.

My full-stack development background (Laravel/PHP) gives me a distinct advantage in security: I understand how applications are architected, how data flows through systems, and where they're most likely to be exploited. I hold a BSc in Information Technology from Ambo University.
